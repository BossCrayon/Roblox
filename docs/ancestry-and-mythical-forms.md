# Ancestry and mythical manifestations

All supported pathways retain their activation and ongoing form VFX. Ending effects now use Spirit worms, Time clock seals, or Star door seals respectively. Forms last at most 60 seconds, then have 45 seconds of recovery. Incomplete forms still impose sanity strain and can end early through loss of control or death. The timer and all numerical values are gameplay balance, not novel claims.

Exposure is server-authoritative: within 90 studs, facing toward the source, and a raycast without intervening walls. Character facing approximates gaze; this does not measure the client's exact camera or screen. Equal or stronger sequences are unaffected. Weaker observers take 0.8% of their maximum health and 0.8 raw sanity per second per sequence gap. Incomplete forms halve those rates. Existing damage defenses and sanity resistance still apply; spawn ForceFields protect players. Uninitiated observers count as rank 10. NPC enemies are included; owned summons are excluded.

B → Character displays derived attributes, ancestry modifiers and the G special skill. Ancestry is a once-per-profile choice with an explicit second confirmation, allowed outside combat. Old profiles default to Human with a choice available. Stats are re-derived on load and advancement, never trusted from stored maxima. Choosing preserves resource percentages; it cannot refill resources or be rerolled.

Human, Sanguine, Giant and Elf are playable ancestry adaptations. They do not grant pathway sequences. The passives, skills and cross-pathway availability are game design, not claims that every canonical member of a race possesses these abilities. Sanguine regeneration and Giant protection use timed server effects; Elf movement respects the normal speed ceiling. Each skill has a server-enforced cost and cooldown.

Research limitation: the connected web search returned errors and direct access to the LOTM Fandom wiki was denied by the environment proxy. Lore could not be independently verified in this update. Reference pages to verify before claiming canon completeness: Mythical Creature Form, Sanguine, Giants and Elves on lordofthemysteries.fandom.com.

Validation covers migration, invalid saved ancestry, derived stats, selection replay, costs, cooldowns, all supported form stages, sequence damage order, tick scaling, and mocked server visibility. Studio rendering and multiplayer playtesting remain necessary.
