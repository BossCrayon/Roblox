# Tingen reconstruction evidence

The user's 1080×809 settings-book photograph is the layout authority. Its 67 numbers are points of interest, not a census of buildings. It shows dense subdivisions, narrow streets, larger institutional blocks, and southern industry. Landmark centers remain tied to those photograph coordinates; footprint dimensions, heights and street offsets are reconstructed. Four studs per image pixel is a gameplay scale. A photograph of a printed 1:10000 map does not itself establish meters per pixel.

Novel-era geography takes precedence when adaptations differ. Fanfiction and game-wiki pages with contradictory location information were not used as canon. The River and Sea Cathedral's precise site and façade remain unconfirmed. Its northern placement is marked approximate. The other cathedral models are generic interpretations, not documented replicas of Saint Selena.

The Khoy River now follows only the visible northeastern reach instead of the prior invented arc around the eastern campuses. Its continuation outside that visible reach is unresolved. Tussock banks follow a rough polygon trace of the photograph. Raster strips provide native-part banks and a shallow bed; this is not Roblox Terrain swimming water.

Unnamed houses use deterministic parcels alongside streets, with small gaps, varied masonry, roofs, windows and chimneys. These are reconstructed buildings, not individually identified canon addresses. The map remains an approximation, despite the more faithful density and better-supported landmark features.
